Information

Venue

The Colony Club, 2310 Park Ave, Detroit, MI. Our ceremony and reception will both be held at this beautiful historic venue in downtown Detroit.

Hotels

We recommend staying at a hotel in Detroit, espcerially if you plan on coming to the After Party. We plan on staying at MGM Grand, but there are plenty of hotels nearby. There are also some AirBnB/VRBOs that can accommodate large parties around Detroit.

Dress Code

Formal and Fun! Wear your favorite suit, dress, jumpsuit, or any elegant ensemble. Please avoid red as that is reserved for the bride.

Gifts

Your presence is the best gift! Please no physical gifts, as cannot take them back to SF. Red Envelopes and Cards are appreciated, but not expected.

Parking

Parking is available near The Colony Club. Please use this QR code to get free parking at the Comerica Garage (50 W. Wontcalm St, Detroit). Guests are also welcome to park overnight.

Guest Policy

To help us keep our celebration intimate, we’re inviting only those named on the invitation. Thank you for understanding!

What to do in Detroit

On Sunday, May 24th, we plan to go to the Henry Ford Museum and Greenfield Village. If you’re interested in joining us, please RSVP on Partiful!

Other things to do while you're in town:

  • The Detroit Riverwalk is really nice and was recently expanded.
  • DIA (Detroit Institute of Arts).
  • Dequindre Cut — a great urban bike/walking trail.
  • Eastern Market.
  • Try the two Coneys (American Coney Island & Lafayette).
  • Downtown, Royal Oak, and Ferndale have many great restaurants and bars.
